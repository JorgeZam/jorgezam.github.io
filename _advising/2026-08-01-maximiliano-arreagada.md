### Overview

The S-MAD web platform (Biometrics and Morphing Detection Demonstration) is designed as an interactive, modular environment for evaluating the resilience of face recognition systems against identity impersonation attacks. The platform is intended to be presented at outreach exhibitions, where visitors can interact with it directly.
