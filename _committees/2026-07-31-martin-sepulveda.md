### Overview

This thesis investigates the use of eye tracking, semantic image analysis, and deep learning to study visual attention and emotional perception in advertising. The results show limited agreement between intended and perceived emotions, highlighting the potential of this integrated framework while emphasizing the need for larger datasets.
